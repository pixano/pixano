In the new workspace UI, deleting a model's annotation is its Reject, and every delete
control says so: on a model's annotation, whatever its review status, the image toolbar's
trash button reads "Reject selected" and the 3D box's HUD "Reject", their tooltip saying that
rejecting deletes it. Before Save, only Discard brings a rejected annotation back, with the
record's other unsaved changes; once saved, a Reject leaves no trace.
