In the legacy video workspace, an object's card offers Track to the end on a frame where the
object has a box or a mask: the tracking job follows it to the video's end in the background,
proposing what the list beside the button says — Masks, Boxes, or Masks and boxes. The card counts
the frames done, and once the job is done, Show the masks (or the boxes, or both) displays what it
wrote, pending review, on the frame after the one the object was tracked from.
