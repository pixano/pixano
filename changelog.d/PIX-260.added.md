The explorer of the legacy app gets an Overview, beside the gallery and the table: a card per field
of the records, per image property and per statistic, the objects counted by class, each a chart
of its values over the records the filters keep, in the theme's colors. Clicking a bar of a card of
the records' own values filters the records on it, every other card following; the card clicked
keeps its bars, those its filter keeps standing out, and clicking the bar again, or Clear, drops
the filter. Each card lists its values in a table, with a button to filter on each. Compare splits
draws each split's share of its values, in a color that stays from card to card, and outlines
where the splits differ. Where the default statistics are not computed yet, the overview says so,
and where their job can run, Read again brings them in once the job an import queued is done.
