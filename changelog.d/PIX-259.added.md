A dataset has an overview, as Know Your Data shows one: `GET /datasets/{id}/overview` answers
how the records the explorer's filters keep, their images and their objects spread — a card per
field of the records, per statistic computed per record or per object, per image property (width,
height, aspect ratio, format, images per record) and for the objects' classes, each a histogram of
numbers or the count of each value, and, asked with `by_split`, counted per split. It reads the
explorer's `filter`, `q` and `where` as the records list does; a card of a column filtered counts
the records the other filters keep, and says how many of each bar its own filters keep. It says
when each statistic was computed, and lists the default statistics the dataset has not computed
yet.
