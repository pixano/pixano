`GET /datasets/{dataset_id}/review/next` returns the next record holding a model's annotations at a
review status, `pending` by default or `accepted`, whatever their kind, and how many records still
hold some. It goes through the records in id order after the one given in `after`, wrapping round
to the first. It counts only annotations on a view an interface can show for review: an image, a
point cloud or a text. The boxes and masks the legacy video tools store stay pending, and no
interface reviews them yet.
