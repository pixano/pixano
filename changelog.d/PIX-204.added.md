The embeddings job can replace a dataset's embeddings: `replace_existing_embeddings`, which the
new UI's job form asks to confirm, deletes every vector the dataset holds, whatever its model,
before computing the new ones. It is how a dataset moves to another model, and how vectors
computed one per record — by the legacy explorer, or by the embeddings job before it embedded
every image — give way to the per-image ones. A job naming a model the inference does not serve
is refused before it starts, before anything is deleted.
