`pixano-worker` computes statistics: the `statistics` job measures, per record and per object, the
statistics a person composed, or the default ones an import queues, from the dataset's annotations
and without any inference, and writes one column per statistic. A rerun writes the same values over
the same rows and keeps the other statistics' columns; an object deleted since loses its row. A
statistic whose measure is computed otherwise since it was composed, or that measures what the
dataset does not hold, is refused when the job is planned; so is a mask's area, or its share of its
image, which the job does not measure yet — masks are counted.
