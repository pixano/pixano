The explorer of the legacy app searches the embeddings of the model you pick. Beside the search bar,
a selector lists the models a search can read, the default and the searched ones always among them,
once there is another to choose; the URL keeps the choice, opening a record and coming back
included, so a text search and More like this read that model. A URL naming a model the dataset
does not hold searches its default model, and says so. The embeddings popover lists every model of
the dataset with its health and how many media it embedded, makes one of them the default, and
offers the repair of the model searched. The explorer's own computation computes the model searched
on the inference that serves it with the weights its embeddings were computed with, and refuses
otherwise. Where the dataset's description of its embeddings cannot be read entirely, it offers to
start again from nothing — every model's embeddings deleted, after a confirmation —, the only way
out, since the embeddings job refuses such a dataset.
