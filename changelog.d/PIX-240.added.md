In review, the default interface's keyboard goes through a record one annotation at a time: the
annotation under review is shown alone on the image, `Enter` accepts it and `Delete` (`Backspace` on
a Mac keyboard) rejects it, and the next one comes under review. Once nothing is left, `Enter`
offers to save and go to the next record, and a second `Enter` does. In review, a click on a box
selects it without editing it: `E` and the pencil still edit.
