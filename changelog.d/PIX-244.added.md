Statistics people compose, without code: a statistic counts an annotation kind or a dataset's
objects, per record or per object, or measures the area of boxes and masks in pixels or as a share
of their image, with optional filters on an object attribute, the annotation's origin and its review
status. The library keeps them for every dataset they apply to: `POST /statistics/definitions`
composes one, `GET /statistics/definitions` lists the library's, and
`GET /datasets/{id}/statistics/choices` and `GET /datasets/{id}/statistics/definitions` say what a
dataset offers to compose from and which of the library's apply to it.
