The embeddings job computes one vector per image of a record — six for a six-camera record —
for the media types chosen in the job form, images by default; a type the job cannot embed
refuses the job. Similarity search ranks a record by its closest image, and "similar to" searches
with every image of the record. The explorer counts embedded images, not records (`total_media`
in `GET /datasets/{id}/filters`). Embeddings a dataset already holds one per record — computed
by the legacy explorer, or by the embeddings job before this change — stay beside the job's
per-image ones.
