An embeddings job ends by indexing the vectors it wrote. From 50,000 vectors, a dataset's
similarity search goes through a vector index and answers in about ten milliseconds, where the
exact search grows with the dataset (61 ms at 100,000 vectors, 162 ms at 300,000); below, the
search stays exact. A table the worker creates gets an `IVF_SQ` index; one computed before keeps
the `IVF_PQ` its descriptor names, slower to train, until the table is dropped and a worker
computes it anew.
