An embeddings job writes its vectors into its model's own table, a model being its name and the
checkpoint the inference serves it with, asked right after each call: a dataset holding another
model's embeddings no longer refuses it, and weights redeployed during a job get a table of their
own. When the inference cannot tell its checkpoint, no checkpoint's vectors are emptied or written
by guess: the job, or the chunk, is refused or tried again later. A dataset whose `embeddings.json`
cannot be read entirely refuses the job when it is planned, before any vector is computed. A
worker opens again a dataset another process changed, so its jobs see the models another worker
computed.
