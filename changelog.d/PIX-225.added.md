In the new workspace UI, the Review tab has "Save & next": it saves the record, then opens the
next one holding annotations of the chosen status — pending by default — and says how many
records hold such annotations. It stays on the record, and says why, when the save fails, when
work is still unsaved, or when no other record is left. No annotation is accepted or rejected
while it runs; opening a record from the list stops it.
