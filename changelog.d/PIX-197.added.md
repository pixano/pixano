The local stack's inference (`docker-compose.inference.yml`) also serves SAM 2.1 tiny and
small on CPU. With MobileCLIP2-S2 and YOLO26n, it reserves 8 CPUs, and does not start with
fewer. It serves no tracking model: tracking by detection needs another inference server, or
two more CPUs and the YOLO plugin's `YOLOByteTrackModel` added to
`docker/inference/models.cpu.py`.
