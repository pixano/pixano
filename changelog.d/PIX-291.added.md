The app answers two questions apart, for the probes of a deployment. `GET /health/live` says that
the process is alive, and tests nothing else. `GET /health/ready` tests what the app depends on —
the storage of the library, and PostgreSQL where `PIXANO_DATABASE_URL` is set — and answers 503
with the name of what is missing until all of it answers, each within 2 s. What a deployment does
not configure is reported as not configured, and fails nothing. The inference never fails the
readiness, because the app serves everything that needs no model without it: an inference that
does not answer is named under `degraded`, and the app stays ready. The app is ready again as soon
as what was missing is back, without a restart. `GET /health` is unchanged: it still tests nothing.
