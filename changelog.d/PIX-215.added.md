A `segmentation` job outlines the boxes already on a dataset's images, a detection's or a
person's: it runs the segmentation model the inference serves (SAM 2.1 on the local stack) on
each image's boxes and writes one mask per box, on the box's object, pending review. A dataset
without masks gains its masks table. A rerun of the same model replaces its own masks still to
review, and another model adds its own; no mask goes on an object a person outlined or whose mask
they accepted, nor over such a mask. `replace_previous`, which the job form asks to confirm, first
deletes every model's mask still to review. A dataset imported by URI, which records no image
size, is read for it.
