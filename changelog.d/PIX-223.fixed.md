In the new workspace UI, a 3D box moved in its panel and not yet applied, or being dragged by
one of its handles, counts as unsaved work: the unsaved count shows it, and Discard closes the
panel and puts the box back. A panel showing a box that changes under it — put back by
Discard, or deleted — closes instead of offering to apply the old move again. The panel's
button that keeps the box with the record's unsaved changes now reads "Apply".
